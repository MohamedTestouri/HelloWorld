## 1. Tunisia — obligations that apply first (owner lives in Tunisia)

### 1.1 Operating legally as a freelancer/services provider

- **Auto-entrepreneur regime** (décret-loi 2020-33): turnover cap **75,000 TND/yr** (services), tax **0.5% of turnover** (services), quarterly declarations, registered via **autoentrepreneur.tn**, 10 TND registration fee; year 1 contributions covered by Fonds National de l'Emploi; 4+3 year duration [V] [1][2].
  - **Key limit:** 75,000 TND/yr ≈ $24–25K/yr — this caps roughly at **~$2,000/mo revenue**, far below the $5,000/mo personal target. Beyond it: patente (progressive IRPP 15–35%) or SARL [V] [1].
- **TVA (VAT):** 19% standard rate; foreign-client services need fact-specific export/zero-rating analysis — **do not assume services to foreigners are automatically VAT-free** [V] [3].
- **CNSS:** registration mandatory from first dinar; non-artisan contribution ~233 TND/quarter under auto-entrepreneur [V] [2][1].
- **2025 Labor Code reform (Law 9-2025):** relevant only if hiring — CDI default, strict outsourcing rules [V] [4]. Not applicable yet (solo).

### 1.2 Getting paid from abroad — the real chokepoint

- **PayPal does not work in Tunisia** for practical purposes; **Payoneer** is the common freelancer rail (receive USD/EUR, withdraw to Tunisian bank) [V] [5][6].
- **Wise:** widely used but with Tunisia limitations; community reports of account freezes [V, community] [5][6].
- **2026 Finance Law / new Foreign Exchange Code:** reportedly ends blanket prohibition on residents holding foreign currency, lets registered freelancers/exporters/labelled startups open **foreign-currency accounts**, and creates a legal basis for PayPal/Stripe subject to Central Bank circulars [E, secondary source — **verify with Central Bank/DGI before relying**] [5].
- **CRS:** Tunisia participates in CRS; foreign account balances are reported to Tunisian tax authorities. Community claims of September 2026 start for Tunisia [H, community source — verify] [7]. **Implication: all foreign income must be declared. No concealment, ever.**
- **Technology card (carte technologique):** sanctioned prepaid card for approved tech spending (hosting, domains, ads) with low annual limits [V] [5].

**Action rule:** until a legal entity + compliant account exists, the only recommended rail is **Payoneer → Tunisian bank, income declared under the chosen Tunisian regime**. Any offshore-account shortcut is prohibited by owner rules.

### 1.3 Tunisian Startup Act — worth evaluating later

Labelling a startup unlocks **special foreign-currency accounts** payable to AWS/Google/Meta without prior Central Bank authorization, plus tax/social incentives; obligations include annual statements by March 31 and >50% natural-person shareholding; company <8 years old, <100 employees, <15M TND turnover [V] [8]. **Candidate structure for Phase 5**, requires accountant + possibly legal counsel.

**Critical distinctions (per master prompt §24):** a foreign company, foreign bank account, or second passport does **not** remove Tunisian tax obligations while the owner is tax-resident in Tunisia. **Recommended sequence:** auto-entrepreneur (now) → accountant review at $2K/mo → SARL *or* UK Ltd at $3–5K/mo (whichever the accountant models as cheaper/compliant) → revisit free-zone structures only at $10K+/mo.
