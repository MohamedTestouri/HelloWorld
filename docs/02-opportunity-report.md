# 02 — Ranked Opportunity Report

*Phase 1 deliverable. Compiled 2026-11-06 from live web research. Evidence quality is marked: **[V]** verified from source, **[E]** estimate, **[H]** hypothesis to validate.*

## Scoring method

12 criteria × 1–5 scale (5 = best), weighted equally → total /60. Scores reflect **this owner's** constraints: Tunisia-based, ~$0 authorized capital, software + VoIP background, no existing audience, needs first revenue fast.

### #3 — Niche micro-SaaS (vertical workflow tool) — **38/60**

Evidence: median micro-SaaS earns ~$500/mo; 70% never break $1,000/mo [E] [8]; vertical CRMs $5K–30K MRR possible for winners [E] [9]; solo examples exist (Closet Tools $38K MRR [V, self-reported] [9]).

| Weak criteria | Why |
|---|---|
| Time to revenue (2) | Weeks–months of build before any validation signal |
| Customer acquisition (2) | Zero distribution; SEO/content takes 6–12 months |
| Competition (2) | Idea lists are copied within weeks |

**Verdict:** do **not** start here. Revisit in Phase 4–5 with revenue + audience from #1.

### #4 — Freelance contracting on marketplaces (Upwork/Malt) — **40/60**

Fastest possible cash (days–weeks), $0 capital, legal via auto-entrepreneur [V] [10]. But: capped income per hour, no leverage, platform risk. **Role:** bridge income during validation, not the end state. Scored high on time-to-revenue (5) and capital (5), low on scalability (2) and recurring revenue (2).

### #5 — Grenada CBI / citizenship programs — **deferred, not an opportunity**

Minimums: NTF $200K (family ≤4) per one source [V, official schedule cited] [11] vs $235K single + $270K real estate per other 2025–26 guides [V] [12][13]; official CBI site snippet shows $150K [14] — **figures conflict and must be re-verified against the official Grenada Schedule of Fees before any decision**. At $0 business capital this is years away. No action, no agent money movement. Tracked in `03-international-strategy.md` §5.

## Demand-validation plan for #1 (before any build)

1. Pick one niche vertical (candidates: dental clinics, law firms, real-estate agencies, e-commerce support — **to be selected via competitor-review mining**).
2. Mine 50+ competitor reviews/complaints for unmet needs.
3. Draft one productized offer: e.g., *"AI lead-intake + follow-up automation — $1,500 build, $500/mo care plan"* (price anchor from [3] [E]).
4. Build a one-page landing site (free hosting) + 3 case-style demo artifacts using synthetic data.
5. Send 100 personalized outreach messages (after owner approves list & copy).
6. Success gate: **≥5 positive replies or ≥1 paid pilot in 30 days** → proceed. Otherwise re-niche.
