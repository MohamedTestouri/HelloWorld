# Productized Offer — Draft v1 (Task A3)

*Status: DRAFT — requires owner approval before publication (A7 gate). Prices are hypotheses to validate in outreach [H].*

## The offer

**Dental Answering Engine — after-hours & overflow AI reception for dental practices.**

> Your practice misses 1 in 3 calls [1]. We install an AI phone receptionist that answers every call after hours and when your front desk is busy, books appointments into your existing calendar, and hands off to a human in one tap. Flat monthly price. Month-to-month. Live in 48 hours.

## Package (v1)

| Component | Scope |
|---|---|
| Answering | After-hours + overflow (rings after N seconds), 24/7 |
| Booking | Writes into practice's existing scheduling link/calendar |
| FAQ | Hours, location, accepted insurance categories, services list (practice-configured) |
| Handoff | Immediate human callback request; "talk to a person" always available (complaint T2) |
| Lead capture | Caller name/number/reason → SMS + email to practice owner; every lead owned (T3) |
| Reporting | Weekly call log: answered, booked, callbacks, abandonment |

**Explicitly out of scope v1:** clinical advice, triage, insurance verification, storing any health information (HIPAA-scope mitigation per docs/08 §2).

## Pricing hypotheses [H] — to be tested, not published until approved

| Tier | Price/mo | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $249 | After-hours only, ≤100 calls/mo |
| Standard | $499 | After-hours + overflow, ≤300 calls/mo, weekly report |
| Setup | $0 (intro offer) / $500 (list price) | First 25 practices get free setup for testimonials |

Anchors: market bands $49–299/mo for SMB receptionists [2]; agency resell $400–600/mo [3]. Undercut on setup, compete on 48h onboarding + human handoff (our differentiators vs T1–T6 complaints).

## Success gates (Phase 3)

1. Landing page live → measure visits (not a success metric by itself).
2. 100 personalized outreach → **≥5 positive replies or ≥1 paid pilot in 30 days** → continue.
3. 0 replies after 100 → re-niche to home-services (docs/08 fallback) with same engine.
