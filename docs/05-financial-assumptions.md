## 3. Scenarios to $5,000 personal net/month

Reminder (from 03): personal net $5K requires roughly **$6.5–8K/mo revenue** after tax/operating costs at current Tunisian regimes [E].

### Scenario A — Service ladder (base case)

| Milestone | Revenue mix | Monthly revenue [E] | Personal net [E] | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | 1 build | $2,000 one-time | ~$1,800 one-time | First paid pilot |
| M2–3 | 2 care plans + 1 build | $3,500 | ~$2,500 | Retention >80% |
| M4–6 | 4–5 care plans + builds | $6,000–7,500 | ~$4,000–5,000 | Outbound engine steady |
| M7–12 | 6–8 care plans + voice upsell | $9,000–12,000 | ~$6,000–8,000 | Voice AI offer (#2) attached |

**Key assumption [H]:** 2–5% positive-reply rate on personalized outreach, 10–20% close rate on positive replies. **To be measured from actual campaign data in Phase 3 — if measured rates are lower, volumes double.**

### Scenario B — Marketplace bridge (if outreach is slow)

Upwork/Malt contracts at $30–60/hr [E], 20–30 hrs/week → $2,600–5,200/mo gross. Slower scaling, but de-risks cash flow. Used only as bridge.

### Scenario C — Product-led (deferred)

Micro-SaaS: excluded from near-term plan (median $500/mo, 70% never reach $1k [3]).

- Personal distributions logged separately from business cash.
